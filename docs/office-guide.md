# Office guide

Open `/office/login`. Type the email and password for your login, then click **Sign in**. The office uses US English. The public site keeps the wording it already has.

The top of every office screen shows your email, your role, and **Sign out**.

## What each role sees

| Role | Links |
|---|---|
| Owner | Home, Requests, Users, Rates, Tours, Places, Blog, Gallery, Reviews, Home slides, Site details |
| Manager | Home, Requests, Rates |
| Editor | Home, Requests, Tours, Places, Blog, Gallery, Reviews, Home slides, Site details |

A Manager does not edit public page text. An Editor does not open Rates. Only the Owner creates a login, removes a login, or changes a role.

## Requests

Open **Requests**. Each row shows the guest, the kind (Contact, Custom trip, or Booking), and the status (New, In progress, or Closed). The message is plain text.

The Owner and the Manager choose a status and click **Save status**. Only the Owner sees **Delete**. An Editor can read the list and does not see those buttons.

## Rates

Open **Rates**. **Save season** sets 15 percent or 20 percent for guest quotes. A quote form on this page can use a different season for that one quote.

**Hotel rate** and **Save hotel** change a stored rate. **Save vehicle** turns a vehicle on or off. **Jeep lines** and **Save jeep** change a jeep line.

## One night and one day on a quote

On **Rates**, use the **Quote** form.

1. Leave Place on Skardu Valley, Mode on Road, Start on Islamabad, and Grade on Deluxe.
2. Leave Vehicle as Gli Car, Days as 5, and Adults as 2.
3. In **Night number**, type `2`. In **Night rate**, type the rupee amount for that night only. In **Night hotel**, type the hotel name for that night.
4. In **Day**, type `3`. In **That day**, choose **No vehicle**.
5. Click **Save quote**.

Night 2 changes only that night. Day 3 drops that day's vehicle rent. The hotel rate list and the saved season stay as they were. A red line under the button means the quote was not saved.

To put a jeep on one day, choose **Jeep** under **That day** and fill **Jeep rent** and **Jeep fuel**. To use a higher vehicle, choose **Higher vehicle** and pick the vehicle.

## Users

Only the Owner opens **Users**. Under **Create a login**, type an email and a password, choose Editor or Manager, and click **Create login**. **Save role** changes a role. **Remove** deletes a login. The Owner cannot remove their own login here.

## Public pages an Editor can change

**Tours**, **Places**, **Blog**, **Gallery**, **Reviews**, **Home slides**, and **Site details** edit the public site. Open a tour and click **Save tour** only when the new title or photo should go live.
